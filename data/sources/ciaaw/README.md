# CIAAW atomic weights

- **`abridged_standard_atomic_weights_2024.tsv`**: Z, symbol, English name, abridged standard
  atomic weight, its uncertainty and the CIAAW notes (g, m, r) for all 118 elements; empty weight
  for elements without a standard atomic weight. From *Abridged Standard Atomic Weights 2024*,
  Commission on Isotopic Abundances and Atomic Weights (IUPAC),
  https://www.ciaaw.org/abridged-atomic-weights.htm, retrieved 2026-11-24 (based on "Atomic
  Weights 2021", with the 2024 revisions of Gd, Lu and Zr). Abridged values are quoted to five
  significant figures unless isotopic variability prevents it.
- **`radioactive_most_stable_isotopes.tsv`**: mass number and half-life of the most stable known
  isotope of each element without a standard atomic weight, as compiled by CIAAW from NUBASE2020
  (Kondev et al., Chinese Physics C 45 (2021) 030001), https://www.ciaaw.org/radioactive-elements.htm
  (last updated 2022-03-29), retrieved 2026-11-24. Where CIAAW lists two isotopes as equally
  stable, both rows are kept; the generator uses the first listed.
